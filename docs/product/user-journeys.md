# Product User Journeys — School OS Multi-Role Platform

## 1. Teacher Persona (Guru)
```text
Login
  │
  ▼
Learning Workspace (/dashboard/learning)
  ├── Unggah Materi (PDF / Video)
  ├── Menyusun & Dipublikasikan Lesson Modul
  └── Membuat Assignment (+ Due Date)
        │
        ▼
Assignment Operations (/dashboard/learning/assignments)
  ├── Review Submissions Siswa (PR / Inbox Style)
  └── Memberi Nilai & Catatan Feedback
        │
        ▼
Assessment Workspace (/dashboard/learning/assessments)
  ├── Memantau Hasil Auto-Graded Kuis
  └── Memeriksa & Mempublikasikan Spreadsheet GradeBook
```

---

## 2. Parent Persona (Orang Tua / Guardian)
```text
Login Parent Portal (/parent)
  │
  ├── Summary Card (Kehadiran %, Jumlah Tugas, Rata-rata Nilai)
  ├── Student Activity Timeline (Event log pengerjaan tugas & kuis)
  └── Unduh Rapor Akademik Resmi (PDF Output)
```

---

## 3. Executive Principal Persona (Kepala Sekolah)
```text
Login Executive Dashboard (/dashboard/analytics)
  │
  ├── School Overview (Statistik Lulus, Total Guru & Siswa, Rata-rata Nilai)
  └── Drill-down Hierarchy (Sekolah ➔ Angkatan ➔ Kelas ➔ Mata Pelajaran ➔ Siswa)
```
